Job Intel

One-time setup

Your profile and CV are stored only in this browser. They are never uploaded and are not part of the published site.

  1. On your laptop, in your job-intel folder, run:
    node export-config.mjs --save
  2. Open the generated my-config.json and copy everything.
  3. Paste it below.

Paste a job description

On the LinkedIn job page click "See more", then select all (Ctrl+A), copy (Ctrl+C) and paste below.
